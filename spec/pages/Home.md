## **🎨 Page Style Requirements**

- Dark theme with subtle grey contrasts
- Minimalistic, editorial-style typography
- Large spacing and generous vertical rhythm
- Sans-serif font, medium weight, slightly condensed appearance
- Soft card surfaces with rounded corners (md or lg radius)
- Hover states should be elegant and subtle
- Maintain all alignment and spacing consistent with the reference
